Entry for the [make.fun competition "Fortune in a Pot"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-golden-heron-44-fortune-in-a-pot-ded981e9): one line, at most 120 characters, telling a houseplant's fortune — kind, funny, and unmistakably about the plant on the windowsill.

## The line

> The plant across the street will envy your window by spring, and you will pretend not to notice.

96 characters. The plant on this sill has a rival across the street, and the joke lands on the last word: the plant is worth envying, and polite enough to pretend it hasn't noticed. The lane (rival-plant envy) was empty in a live scan of 107 submissions, while the sun, the new leaf and outgrowing the pot were all taken many times over. The line was picked by a ten-candidate round-robin tournament judged on the competition brief's exact criterion order (9–0); full record in `CANDIDATES.md` in the build directory.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`), no trailing newline. Python 3.13, standard library only, deterministic — no clock, no randomness, no network.
